## Overview

The site is a static build. Pages are plain templates, projects and posts are MDX content collections, and the scripts are small ES modules loaded once and re-bound after each client-side navigation.

## How it was built

I worked iteratively with an AI assistant: describe the change, look at the result in the browser, then refine. The assistant wrote and edited code; I made the design calls, checked behaviour in the browser and kept the content honest. Typical loops were visual tweaks (stroke weight of the title, where the shots launch from), behaviour fixes (animations that broke after page changes) and clean-up (TypeScript config, dead code).
